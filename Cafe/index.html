THE BAROAK STORY

A room designed to make you stay.

BAROAK takes its name from two inspirations: the intricate detail and structure of Baroque design, and the warmth and simplicity of oak. The result is a considered space that feels elegant without becoming intimidating.

The destination moves between a classic, layered indoor setting and a relaxed open-air garden. Its multi-cuisine kitchen draws from Indian, Oriental and Continental influences, while the bar reworks global classics with Indian botanicals, herbs and spices.
